### he Big Picture: The House Price Factory

Imagine you own a factory. Your factory has one job: **Guess the price of a house.**

You built a giant conveyor belt with **3 Stations**. This conveyor belt is what we call a **Pipeline**.

#### Station 1: The Sorting Table (`ColumnTransformer`)

Raw houses come in. They are messy. Some info is numbers (area, rooms), some info is words (city, condition).

- The Sorting Table pushes the **Numbers to the Left Belt**.
- It pushes the **Words to the Right Belt**.

#### Station 2: The Cleaners (`Imputer`, `Scaler`, `OneHotEncoder`)

The belts move forward. The machines clean the data so the Brain can understand it.

- **Left Belt (Numbers):** A machine fills in missing numbers. Another machine shrinks big numbers (like area=200) and stretches small numbers (like rooms=3) so they are all the same size.
- **Right Belt (Words):** Computers can't read "Casablanca". So a machine turns words into **Lightbulbs**.
  - If the house is in Casablanca, the Casablanca bulb turns ON (1), and all other city bulbs turn OFF (0).

#### Station 3: The Brain (`LinearRegression`)

Now, the data is perfectly clean. Just numbers and lightbulbs. The Brain looks at your 5,500 training houses. It tries to find the **Secret Recipe**. It thinks: *"Hmm... maybe 1 m² is worth 10,000 MAD? And if the Casablanca bulb is ON, I add 500,000 MAD?"* It guesses, checks its mistake, adjusts the recipe, and guesses again. It does this millions of times in one second until the recipe is perfect.

---

### 🎮 What is Python doing?

Python is just the **Factory Manager**. You use Python words to give orders to the factory:

1. **`model.fit(X_train, y_train)`**
   - **Translation:** "Manager, turn on the factory! Let the 5,500 houses go through the 3 stations so the Brain can learn the Secret Recipe."
   - *This is the STUDY phase.*
2. **`model.predict(X_test)`**
   - **Translation:** "Manager, here are 1,400 NEW houses. Send them through the EXACT SAME 3 stations, and let the Brain use the Secret Recipe to guess their prices."
   - *This is the EXAM phase.*

That's it. `fit` = study. `predict` = take the exam. The Pipeline is just the factory belt that makes sure the data is cleaned the exact same way every time.
